01The mandate

Statement of intent: Head of Marketing search for Wanteeed

The mandate you're opening today doesn't just call for an excellent marketer. It calls for a leader capable of reading an organization, making structural decisions, and bringing teams along in the same direction.

These profiles exist.

They rarely respond to freelance postings.

02Conviction

Our conviction

Marketing skills probably won't be the limiting factor in this search. The real challenge will be finding someone who combines four qualities rarely found together:

01

Exceptional analytical ability

Reading the acquisition → activation → retention funnel and unit economics (CAC, LTV) before prioritizing anything.

02

Judgment to arbitrate between strategy, organization, and execution

Balancing setting the strategy, framing the brand work underway, and driving execution — without spreading too thin across all three at once.

03

Enough credibility to build trust quickly

Coordinating a team that's already in place, plus outside reinforcements, requires legitimacy that isn't handed over — it has to be earned fast.

04

The ability to build a function, not just produce results

Putting in place indicators and rituals built to last, with impact measured in activation and incremental revenue — not leads or brand awareness.

That's precisely what makes this search complex.

03Approach

How we'd approach this case

— 01

Getting the balance right across dimensions

The mandate combines strategic analysis, leadership, management, change management, and operational expertise. We work to clarify the real weight of each of these dimensions, and to determine what the Head of Marketing should own directly versus what should be supported by complementary expertise.

— 02

Finding the rare profiles

These profiles rarely respond to postings. We approach them directly, assess them through interviews, personalized evaluations, and detailed briefings on your mandate, and only present candidates who have fully convinced our sector experts.

— 03

Validating the best match with key stakeholders

Skills aren't enough. We assess fit between the candidate, the founders, and the teams through personalized interviews and personality assessments.
